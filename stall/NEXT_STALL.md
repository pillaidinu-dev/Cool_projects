## Setup used

- Menu at the end of the first stall: Medhu Vadai $6 (1 plate), Paneer Puff $4 (1 piece), Podi Idly $8 (1 plate), Rose Milk $3 (10 oz), Misc $3.
- Payments: cash and Zelle.
- Planned spend: stall fee $60, supplies $50, cooking items $50 ($160). Actual: $60 + $38 + $45 + $1 "Drift" = $144.
- Stock made: Vadai 50, Puff 30, Idly 20, Rose Milk 15.

## First stall results (one evening, recorded Oct 11–12, 2026)

- 31 orders, 54 items, **$271 sales**, $144 spend, **$127 profit**, average order $8.74.
- About 2 hours 35 minutes from first to last sale; broke even about 1 hour 50 minutes in.
- By item: Medhu Vadai $96 (16 sold of 50), Paneer Puff $84 (21 of 30, most units), Podi Idly $64 (8 of 20), Rose Milk $24 (8 of 15), Misc $3 (1).
- Payments: Zelle $212 (23 orders, 78%), cash $59 (8 orders).

## Lessons for next time

- Paneer Puff sold the most units and came closest to selling out: make more.
- About two-thirds of the Vadai was left (34 of 50): make fewer, or sell it earlier or in a combo.
- Most customers paid by Zelle: keep the Zelle QR code visible; a small cash float is enough.
- The projection guesses (160 items over two days) were for a full weekend. One evening sold 54, so plan for roughly 50–60 items per evening at a similar venue.

## Before the next stall

1. In the tracker, use **Clear sales and tips** (bottom of the page) to start fresh. Export a CSV first if you want to keep the old sales.
2. Update prices, stock and spend (Edit menu, Stock section, Spend).
3. Do a dry run on each phone, then clear it again.
4. Ask Claude to refresh the projection workbook's inputs, and afterwards to fill the Actuals tab and make a new summary chart.
